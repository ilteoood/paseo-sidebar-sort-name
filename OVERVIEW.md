Sidebar view that groups workspaces by project and adds a Name option to the sort order.

Paseo itself always lists projects alphabetically, but the sidebar's Sort menu only offers Manual, Created, and Last prompted, all of which describe a workspace rather than a name. This view keeps the project grouping and adds Name, so workspaces inside a project are listed as A to Z by workspace name.

Projects stay alphabetical by project name. Workspaces are grouped under their project, pinned workspaces sit in a Pinned section above, and the rows use the same density as the built-in sidebar.

Pick the view in Settings, Sidebar, Sidebar view. Choose the workspace order from the Sort control at the right of the Workspaces header: Name, Created, or Last prompted. The choice is stored on the device, so each client can keep its own order. Paseo 0.16.0 or newer is required.

Manual drag order, project collapsing, context menus, and drag and drop are not available to plugins, so this view does not have them. Workspaces without a project section of their own still appear under their project, and projects with no visible workspace are hidden.
